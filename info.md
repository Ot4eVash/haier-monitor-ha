# Haier Multi-Split Monitor

Calculate electric power, COP/EER/SEER/SCOP, per-room heat distribution, and component health (outdoor coil cleanness, filter, refrigerant) for Haier 2U50S2SM1FA-3 multi-split AC via ESPHome integration — without an external power meter.

**80+ entities**: word-based statuses (`health`, `efficiency_label`, `what_doing`), per-component diagnostics, and an actionable recommendation aggregator.

Tables and physics verified against first-party Service Manual V1 (2021) and peer-reviewed literature.
